# DeskBreak

A personal iOS app that reminds you to drink water and stand up while you work
at your desk. Set two intervals, tap **Start**, lock the phone.

Not on the App Store — build it yourself and sideload it. See
**[docs/INSTALL.md](docs/INSTALL.md)**.

## How it works

The app never runs a background timer. iOS suspends apps seconds after the screen
locks, so instead DeskBreak pre-schedules every reminder for the session as a local
notification and goes to sleep; **iOS** delivers them. That is why the reminders
still arrive after you force-quit the app, and why the app costs no battery while
a session is running.

- Two independent intervals, changeable mid-session
- A different bundled tone per reminder, previewable in Settings
- **Drank it** / **Snooze 5 min** buttons on the alert, so you can act from the
  Lock Screen
- Daily tally of the reminders you actually acted on
- Pause and resume, which shifts the schedule so "45 minutes" means 45 minutes of
  actual desk time
